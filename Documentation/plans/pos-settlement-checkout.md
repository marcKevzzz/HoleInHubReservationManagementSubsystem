# Next implementation plan — POS settlement checkout

1. **Agree the contract with the venue and POS team.** Approve bay release at departure. Obtain sample evidence for unpaid, partial, fully settled, split, voided and refunded bills. Decide how POS maps reservation_id to bills, how evidence is authenticated, which system initiates verification, and how to reconcile outages and late reversals. Deliver a signed-off mapping and a working POS sandbox.
2. **Rehearse MongoDB and compatibility.** Migration 023 is included in this release. Inventory status validators, allocation filters, replacement writers, staff search, customer views, notifications and exports. Verify its intermediate state and optional evidence fields against staging data. Preserve existing completed records. Rehearse migration, rerun and restoration on an isolated replica set before rolling out writers.
3. **Validate the gated reservation implementation with the venue.** Exercise the included End session and Complete checkout commands with synthetic POS evidence. Reuse existing transaction guards, revisions and idempotency checks. Ensure early departure, multi-bay release and attendance corrections maintain consistent occupancy. Require verified, current full settlement for checkout. Prevent a correction from bypassing that gate.
4. **Connect the POS sandbox.** Implement the agreed authenticated exchange. Persist the reservation and bill correlation, outcome, verification time and event identity. Support retry and reconciliation. Test duplicate, delayed and out-of-order events, unavailable POS, partial payments, split bills and a deposit with a balance outstanding.
5. **Update staff workflows and reports.** Show the two actions and timestamps separately, including a searchable list of visits awaiting settlement. Verify customers receive clear booking status and no misleading payment receipt. Update the database dictionary, status definitions and operating procedures.
6. **Verify and release.** Test concurrent checkout and settlement updates, unauthorized access, bay reuse immediately after departure and repeat commands. Example acceptance case: departure at 17:00 releases the bays; a partial payment at 17:05 leaves checkout pending; POS confirms full settlement at 17:12; one staff checkout then completes the visit without changing the departure time. Run the build, formatting, JavaScript, browser, smoke and replica-set suites. Pilot with one venue and reconcile results against POS before general enablement.

**Rollout boundary.** Keep live financial enforcement disabled until the POS sandbox passes the acceptance cases and the venue approves the operating procedure. For rollback after activation, preserve settlement evidence and pending visits for reconciliation; prevent older writers from replacing documents with the new state.
